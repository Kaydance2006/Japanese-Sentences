# Nihongo Express (にほんごエクスプレス)

A beginner Japanese game for kids (about age 10). It practices particles, nouns, verbs, days of the week and how-often words, using the vocabulary from the GENKI cheat sheet and the 50 practice sentences.

**To play:** open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Nothing needs to be installed.

## Stations (games)

| # | Station | What it practices |
|---|---------|-------------------|
| 01 | ことば Picture Words | Places, food and things, verbs (ます form), time words. You see a picture or hear a word and pick the match. |
| 02 | ようび Days of the Week | げつようび to にちようび, their kanji (月 moon, 火 fire …), and which day comes before or after. |
| 03 | どのくらい How Often? | いつも, よく, ときどき, あまり, ぜんぜん, まいにち, まいしゅう, and the rule that あまり and ぜんぜん take 〜ません. |
| 04 | じょし Particle Pop | Fill in は を で に も の か, including "no particle" after きょう and あした. |
| 05 | ぶんをつくろう Sentence Builder | Put word blocks in order (3, then 4, then 5 blocks). Any order is accepted as long as the verb comes last. |
| 06 | きいてね Listen & Pick | Hear a full sentence and pick the matching picture story. |
| 07 | チャレンジ Challenge Ride | A mix from every station. |

## How it keeps questions fresh
- Sentences are built from the word lists (who, when, where, what, verb), so there are thousands of possible sentences. A set of hand-written sentences from the practice sheet covers も, の, questions (か), invitations (〜ませんか) and the past tense (しました).
- The game remembers which questions were already asked and skips them. When a station has used every question, it starts over, but never with the questions from the ride just played.

## Sound and audio
- Japanese speech uses the device's built-in voice (Web Speech API). Every sentence has 🔊 (normal speed) and 🐢 (word by word, with each word highlighted).
- Sound effects are made in the browser, so there are no audio files.
- If no Japanese voice is installed, a note at the top explains how to add one.

## Notes
- Progress (stars, stickers, questions already seen) is saved in the browser's local storage.
- The alcohol sentences (おさけ) from the practice sheet were left out because this is for a child. A kid-friendly drink, ジュース (juice), was added instead.
